SMS from your Android phone
{% if sm.receives and sm.configured %}Sends and receives {% elif sm.configured %}Sends by itself {% else %}One click per SMS{% endif %}
An Android phone with a SIM sends and receives the SMS, from its own number, with the free app SMS Gateway for Android. SMS that candidates send are read by the assistant like emails and WhatsApp messages: it answers alone when it is sure, otherwise it asks you, and it learns from your answers. Without the phone, each SMS waits in the inbox with a button that opens the messages of your phone, written. (An iPhone cannot do this.)
Where the phone sends the SMS it receives (its webhook for sms:received): {{ sm.webhook }}
How to set it up (about 10 minutes)
  1. On the Android phone, install SMS Gateway for Android (sms-gate.app; for this computer's address on the Wi-Fi, the "insecure" version of the app). Open
  2. In the app, switch on Local server: it shows its address, a username and a password. Write them above.
  3. In the app's Settings › Webhooks, copy the signing key and write it above.
  4. Add a webhook for sms:received with the address above (the phone and this computer on the same Wi-Fi, and the application started for the phone).
  5. Press “Test the phone”. Then choose SMS at the top of the inbox if you want SMS to go first.
