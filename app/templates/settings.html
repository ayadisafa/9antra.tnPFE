Today: every reminder page has a WhatsApp button. It opens WhatsApp on the candidate's number with the message already written; you press send. Nothing to set up.
To have reminders go out on WhatsApp by themselves, WhatsApp requires a business number (Meta → WhatsApp Business Platform). Add these lines to the .env file, then restart:
WHATSAPP_TOKEN=the permanent token of your WhatsApp Business app WHATSAPP_PHONE_ID=the identifier of your business phone number WHATSAPP_TEMPLATE=the name of a message WhatsApp approved (one variable: the text)
This is WhatsApp's own rule: a business may only write first with a message it approved beforehand.
